**Topia Bot** is a **chat-native, command-based crypto trading bot on Telegram**, designed for users who want to execute trades instantly right where conversations already happen.

Many modern trading bots, including tools like Bankr, have proven that **text-based trading** is powerful. However, execution speed and interaction design still matter, especially in fast-moving markets.

**Imagine Bankr, but native to Telegram and optimized for speed.**

Most Telegram trading bots today including tools like Sigma, Based Bot, and others. Rely heavily on **button-based interfaces**. While functional, these interfaces introduce friction: multiple clicks, delayed execution, and slower reaction times in fast-moving markets.

Topia Bot removes that friction.

Topia Bot is built around **direct command execution**, allowing users to trade through simple text prompts instead of navigating button-heavy interfaces. With a single line such as:

```
/buy {TOKEN} {amount} ETH
```

### **Chat-Native, Command-Based Trading**

Topia Bot is designed around **command-based interaction**, allowing trades to be executed as naturally as sending a message. This approach eliminates unnecessary steps and enables users to react faster, saving valuable seconds when timing matters.

Because Topia Bot operates directly inside chats, it transforms Telegram from a messaging app into a **live trading environment**.

Users can:

- Buy and sell tokens directly in group chats
- Send tokens to other Telegram handles instantly
- Distribute airdrops on group chats
- Limit Orders and Stop Loss (Via command)
- Trailing Stop Loss (Via command - Coming Soon)
- Multi Chain Supports.
- And many more to come!

This creates a more**interactive trading experience** , where execution happens at the speed of conversation.

### **Built for Speed & Reliability**

Behind the scenes, Topia Bot runs on **fast, dedicated infrastructure** optimized for low-latency command execution and reliable transaction submission.

The result is not just convenience—but **speed, consistency, and performance**, even during high-traffic moments.

### **Platform Today, Vision Tomorrow**

Topia Bot is currently available on **Telegram**, where crypto communities already gather and trade.

In the future, Topia will expand beyond Telegram to include:

- Desktop trading terminals
- Multi-Chain Supports
- Multi Platform Supports (Base App, Farcaster, X, Discord, WhatsApp)

Topia Bot is built to become a **universal trading layer for chat-based crypto execution**.

Topia Bot is a **collaboration project between Based Topi and **[**BOBR**](https://basedbobr.com/), combining shared infrastructure, development experience, and community-driven design.

This collaboration brings together a focus on **fast execution, usability, and social trading**, forming the foundation for Topia Bot’s long-term vision as a scalable, command-based trading platform.
